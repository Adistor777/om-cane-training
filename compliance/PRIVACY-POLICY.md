This policy is written to satisfy the notice requirements of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the DPDP Rules, 2025 (notified 13 November 2025). It is published in English; a Hindi version will be made available.

## 1. What this app is

O&M Cane Training is a research app used by trained teachers at participating schools for the blind to record structured orientation-and-mobility (cane training) assessments of visually impaired children. It is part of a closed research pilot. The app is used **by teachers, not by children**. Children do not operate the app and no data is collected from a child's own device.

## 2. What data is processed, and why

**Child data (entered by the teacher, with guardian consent):** name, date of birth, height, weight, dominant hand, an optional low-resolution photo used only for on-device identification, assessment results for each activity, and — only where a guardian has given specific written consent — short videos of the child performing training activities, filmed by the teacher.

**Guardian consent record:** the consenting guardian's name, relation to the child, the method by which consent was taken, and the dates consent was given or withdrawn. This is kept as evidence of verifiable consent under Rule 10 of the DPDP Rules.

**Teacher and school data:** the logged-in teacher's roster identity and school, stamped on each assessment record for research attribution.

**Purpose:** evaluating cane-training teaching methods and child skill progression across pilot schools, to improve orientation-and-mobility instruction for visually impaired children. Data is not used for advertising, behavioural monitoring, tracking, or profiling of any child, and is never sold.

## 3. Pseudonymisation

Every child is assigned a random research ID at enrolment. Assessment records, video files, and data exports are keyed to this research ID. The default data export contains **no name and no date of birth**; the table linking research IDs to names stays with the school and is exported separately only when explicitly required and authorised.

## 4. Where data is stored

Data is stored **on the teacher's device**, inside the app's private storage, and does not leave the device except when a teacher explicitly exports it for the research team. [PLANNED: when cloud sync is enabled, data will be stored with Supabase on servers located in India, protected by per-school access controls. Update this section before enabling sync.]

## 5. Consent, and withdrawing it

Assessment participation is covered by the guardian consent form signed at enrolment. Video recording requires a **separate, specific consent** and is technically blocked in the app for any child without it. A guardian may withdraw consent at any time by telling the school; withdrawal takes effect immediately, stops all future recording, and — on request — already-stored videos are deleted. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing done before it.

## 6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, a child's parent or legal guardian may: request access to the child's data; request correction of inaccurate data; request erasure of the child's data; withdraw consent; and raise a grievance with the contact named above. If unsatisfied, a complaint may be made to the Data Protection Board of India. Requests can be made through the child's school in person, in writing, or by email, and will be honoured without requiring any technical knowledge.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Data is retained only for the duration of the research pilot and any period required by the research ethics approval, after which it is deleted. Deleting a child from the app erases the child's profile, all assessment records, **and all stored video files**. A full device wipe function removes all data from a device.

## 8. Children's data — additional safeguards

All data subjects in this pilot are children under 18. Accordingly: consent is taken from a parent or legal guardian with identity and relationship recorded (DPDP Rule 10); the app performs no tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children (DPDP Act §9(3)); and processing is limited to the stated research purpose.

## 9. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be notified to participating schools and guardians before they take effect.
