a11y: make a playing sound stoppable from the pad you are already on

Reported by the blind reviewer (2026-07-29): "difficulty pausing the
sounds". Traced, and it was worse than it sounds.

When a sound starts, the teacher's screen-reader cursor is ON the pad
they just tapped. Reaching Pause meant swiping forward through the rest
of the grid, the track name, the group label and the seek bar before
even entering the transport row -- where Play/Pause sat THIRD, behind
Shuffle and Previous. Ten to twenty-five swipes while the sound plays.
And the obvious instinct made it worse: tapping the same pad again
RESTARTED it from zero, because playIdx went straight to load(), which
sets currentTime = 0.

- The pad is now a toggle. Tapping a playing pad stops it -- zero
  navigation, and the convention every media UI already uses. A
  different pad still switches to that sound; only the same pad toggles.
- stopPad() stops and rewinds rather than pausing: for a 3-second drill
  sound, resuming from the middle is never what anyone wants, the child
  needs to hear it whole.
- The pad's NAME tracks state ("Play dog" / "Stop dog"). For a screen
  reader user, discoverability is entirely in the label -- a control
  that says "Play" while playing teaches the wrong thing.
- Play/Pause moved to FIRST in the transport DOM order. Visual row is
  unchanged: styles.css restores shuffle-prev-play-next-repeat with CSS
  order, so play stays centred. Reading order and visual order are
  allowed to differ, and here they should.
- Starting a sound now announces its length once metadata loads
  ("Playing dog, 3 seconds"), so the teacher knows whether to hunt for
  stop or just wait.
- Repeat-one says what it means: "this sound will loop until you stop
  it". It is the only mode with no natural end, and the one where not
  finding pause turns from annoying into stuck.
